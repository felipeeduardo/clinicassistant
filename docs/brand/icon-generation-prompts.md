# Prompts de exploração de ícones

Todos os prompts abaixo pedem símbolo vetorial flat, minimalista, sem texto, sem 3D, sem gradientes excessivos, sem cruz médica dominante, sem copiar WhatsApp/Twilio e legível em favicon 16px.

1. **Conversation + Calendar** — “Create a minimal geometric vector mark combining an abstract conversation bubble, a calendar page and a confirmation check; premium B2B SaaS health-tech; dark navy, primary blue, off-white; no text; no WhatsApp logo.”
2. **IR Monogram** — “Create a distinctive geometric I+R monogram for IA Recepção, a clinic operations SaaS; single continuous silhouette; readable as an app icon; flat vector; dark/light/monochrome variants; no medical cross.”
3. **Smart Reception** — “Create a compact vector symbol showing message → organized schedule → confirmation as three connected shapes; friendly operational health-tech; no robot, brain or chat platform logo.”
4. **Care + Connection** — “Create an abstract symbol for care, patient and clinic connection using two embracing curves around a central point; premium SaaS; no generic medical cross; strong monochrome silhouette.”
5. **Pulse / Flow** — “Create a single continuous line mark representing conversation, agenda and human service with a subtle pulse rhythm; scalable vector; calm B2B health-tech; no ECG literalism.”
